OUR STORY

Welcome to My Favorite Neighbor, where every dish tells a story and every meal feels like home. We believe in creating memorable brunch experiences with fresh, locally-sourced ingredients and innovative flavor combinations.

Our menu is carefully crafted to bring together classic comfort food with exciting new twists. From our signature Ragu Blanco to our iconic handmade brunch dishes, every item is made with love and attention to detail.

Whether you're stopping by for a quick coffee or settling in for a leisurely brunch with friends, we're here for you as your favorite neighbor.
